# Learning objectives

8 learning objectives
A student who has met the objs of the course will be able to:
1. Explain the fundamental of electricity markets
2. Explain various **market, actors**, and **pricing mechanisms**
3. Explain the market-clearing process from both optimisation and equilibrium perspectives
4. Describe the impact of Renewable on electricity markets
5. Develop mathematical models including optimisation and equilibrium problems for clearing various electricity markets
6. Design mathematical models for market participants enabling to make optimal offering strategy decisions under uncertainty.
7. Implement some of these mathematical models in **Julia or Python**
